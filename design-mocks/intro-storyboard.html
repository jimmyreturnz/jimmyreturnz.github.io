Intro storyboard

Power-on → boot → desktop → Lobby. About 4.8 s, Skip visible the whole time. For your review before any code.

0.0 – 0.7 s

1 · Hold the power button

See
Dark navy screen, one glowing power button in a heart-pink ring. Holding fills the ring clockwise (the progress stripe); the button swells to 108%. Release early and the ring drains.
Hear
Low hum that rises in pitch while held (generated).
Input
Mouse: hold 0.7 s. Phone: one tap runs a fast fill. Keyboard: hold Space/Enter. Screen reader: plain "Power on" button.
0.7 – 1.5 s

2 · Explode into ripples

See
The button bursts into 5 SVG rings that expand past the screen edges, staggered by 0.08 s, colour moving pink → lavender → blue, fading as they go. One tiny screen nudge (≤6 px, once).
Hear
Soft boom plus a glassy chime.
Cost
5 circles, scale + opacity only.
1.5 – 2.8 s

3 · Boot with a glitch

See
Black screen. Two short glitch bursts (RGB split + slice jitter) while 4 BIOS lines type fast, then our heart logo and a loading bar fill in ~0.5 s. Glitch ≤3 flashes per second.
Hear
Static crackle on each burst, keyboard-tick on the lines, a two-note chime at the logo.
Text
Fast typing, in Orbitron; lines are placeholders you can change.
2.8 – 3.8 s

4 · Auto login, desktop, arrows

See
A 0.3 s "jimmyreturnz" login flash, then the NSO desktop appears (stripe wallpaper, pixel stickers) and two windows pop in. Three vectorheart arrows streak from bottom-left to top-right.
Hear
Window pops (soft, 2 notes) and an upward swish per arrow.
3.8 – 4.8 s

5 · Wave falls to the floor

See
A wave line crosses the middle, then collapses down into the histogram (about 48 rounded bars) along the bottom of the screen. The NSO windows Flip into the Lobby cards, the stripes dissolve into the vectorheart gradient and the stickers turn into floating hearts. Everything is in place by 4.8 s.
Hear
A rising shimmer; the music fades in at 40% and the bars start to move with it.
after 4.8 s

6 · The Lobby, alive

See
Gradient drifting, circles pulsing, histogram dancing. Hover or tap the avatar and the six glyph-only icons spring out and orbit (one turn per 12–16 s), clickable.
Idle
Two loops only: pulsing circles and the histogram (gradient drift is cheap CSS). Auto-off if frames drop.

Rules that hold for the whole sequence

  • Skip is visible from the first frame and jumps to the finished Lobby with a 0.3 s fade. Skip leaves sound off; the power button turns it on.
  • Once per browser: remembered in localStorage; a "replay intro" link sits in the footer.
  • Reduced motion: a still title card with one "Enter" button, no hold, no glitch.
  • Flash safety: never more than 3 flashes per second (glitch and ripples).
  • Build: one master GSAP timeline with labels, so Skip is a seek; all graphics are SVG; sounds are generated, replaceable by files later.
  • Fonts: Orbitron for boot text, Press Start 2P for window title bars (one swappable token).
