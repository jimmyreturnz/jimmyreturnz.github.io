# jimmyreturnz portfolio

A personal portfolio site that presents its owner as a developer and as a rhythm-game player, in a pastel vectorheart style.

## Visitor journey

**Intro**:
The game-like animation a visitor sees on their first visit, built from vectorheart elements. Always skippable.
_Avoid_: Splash, loading screen, opening

**Lobby**:
Everything before the Portfolio: profile, info, and the Enter button.
_Avoid_: Landing, home, title screen, gate

**Warp**:
The transition that morphs the visitor from the Lobby into the Portfolio.
_Avoid_: Morph, page transition, enter animation

**Portfolio**:
The main page, made of Showcases.
_Avoid_: Main page, dashboard

**Showcase**:
One self-contained block inside the Portfolio (e.g. Mania, Taiko, TETR.IO, Projects, Shoutouts, Blog).
_Avoid_: Section, feature, widget

## Showcases

**Skills and Setups**:
The Showcase listing tech skills alongside gaming gear and settings.
_Avoid_: Loadout, skills card, gear

**Shoutouts**:
The Showcase naming friends and people the owner respects, each with an optional quote.
_Avoid_: Credits, friends list, thanks

**Timeline**:
A secondary, scrollable history covering high school, university, internship, and career only.
_Avoid_: Journey, about timeline, level map

**Stats Card**:
A card showing the owner's live public profile numbers from one platform (osu!, TETR.IO, or GitHub).
_Avoid_: Profile widget, badge

**Guestbook**:
Where visitors leave short messages for the owner, in Thai, English, or Japanese.
_Avoid_: Comments, wall, chat

## Audio

**BGM**:
The background music that plays quietly in the Portfolio.
_Avoid_: Background song, theme music

**Feature audio**:
Any sound a Showcase plays (a chart's song, hitsounds, video audio); while it plays, BGM fades out.
_Avoid_: SFX (reserved for short interface sounds)

**SFX**:
Short interface sounds such as clicks, hovers, and the Warp.
_Avoid_: Hitsound (reserved for chart playback)
