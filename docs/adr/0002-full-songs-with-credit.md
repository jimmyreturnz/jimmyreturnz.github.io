# Ship full songs for the chart players, credited, loaded on demand

The osu!mania and osu!taiko players use the full songs rather than short clips, because the owner's own charts are the point of the Showcases. The site is a non-commercial personal portfolio: no ads, no sales, nothing sold on the pages that play music. Audio is compressed and fetched only when a visitor opens or plays a chart, and every player shows a credit line. If an artist or rights holder objects, the track is removed or cut down immediately.

## Permissions

- **かめりあ – Quaoar**: free to use as long as the use is not illegal; Camellia published the track with a download link himself: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yjQmZBvi3WE
- **DJ SHARPNEL – THE POWER OF UNDERGROUND**: no written statement found; relying on DJ SHARPNEL's long-standing tacit tolerance of fan use, with credit. Weakest of the two; first to cut if challenged.
